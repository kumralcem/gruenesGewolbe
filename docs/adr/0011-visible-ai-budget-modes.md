# Visible AI Budget Modes

AI-assisted capture should be available by default, especially for idea and source captures, but it must be governed by visible budget modes and cost logging. Budget-gated vision calls may be used for image captures and imports to suggest tags, subject, mood, style, and obvious artwork identity, but saving the image must not depend on them. This keeps summarization and tagging useful without letting agentic enrichment, retries, browsing, vision analysis, or high-cost models silently turn normal capture into open-ended spend.
